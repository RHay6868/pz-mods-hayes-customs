# 🎒 Hayes Customs (Build 41 Legacy)

**Build your look. Your gear. Your style.**  
This mod introduces a range of custom wearable content to Project Zomboid, including bags, accessories, unique outfits, and layered gear tailored for variety and expression.

This is the **final release for Build 41**, preserved for multiplayer compatibility and legacy servers.

> ⚠️ Please do not reupload or include this mod in modpacks without permission from the creator.

---

## 🎒 Features

- Custom backpacks and utility bags
- Modular clothing and accessory pieces for unique outfits
- Designed for flexibility and compatibility with other clothing mods

---

## ✅ Compatibility

- ✔️ Compatible with **Project Zomboid Build 41**
- ❌ Not updated for Build 42 (which revamps clothing structure, masks, layering, and outfit definitions)

> A Build 42-compatible version will be maintained on the `main` branch of this repo.

---

## 💾 Manual Installation

1. Download the Build 41 release:  
   👉 [Release vB41.0](https://github.com/RHay6868/pz-mods-hayes-customs/releases/tag/vB41.0)  
   Or [Download ZIP directly](https://github.com/RHay6868/pz-mods-hayes-customs/archive/refs/tags/vB41.0.zip)

2. Extract the folder to your mods directory:

C:\Users\YourUsername\Zomboid\mods\

3. Launch the game and enable the mod in the **Mods** menu

## 📌 Notes

Hayes Customs started as a personal modular gear kit and has grown into a visual customization pack for survivors who want a unique look.  
It plays well with other clothing mods and was built to integrate seamlessly with RP environments, screenshots, or just cool apocalypse fits.

Build 42 support will reflect the new outfit system, item mask logic, and expanded customization layers.
